Our method

Built for clarity, not certainty theatre.

  1. Break down the claim into checkable parts.
  2. Retrieve relevant official documents (planned).
  3. Authenticate publisher and source.
  4. Compare passages against each part.
  5. Explain evidence, uncertainty and safety.
This is an interface prototype. The reference dataset is still a draft; no claim has been promoted to independently reviewed gold status.

Five verdicts

Verified · Direct current primary evidence
Supported · Reliable but incomplete evidence
Unverified · Insufficient evidence
Contradicted · Direct reliable conflict
Outdated · Previously supported, no longer current

This demo intentionally does not assign the first, second, fourth or fifth label without a functioning evidence pipeline.
